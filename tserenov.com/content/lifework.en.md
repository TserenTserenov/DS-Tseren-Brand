My work is about helping people develop the capacity to create: to recognise what needs to change, understand a situation, and turn an intention into a working result.

## Thinking and doing

Systems thinking helps us see connections between a person, their work, a team, and the wider world. Personal development helps us acquire the ways of thinking and acting that a situation demands.

I bring these interests together through writing, community work, and environments that support sustained practice.

## Human agency and AI

AI can extend what a person is capable of doing. Purpose, judgement, responsibility, and meaning remain human work. I’m interested in how we can build that collaboration while retaining the ability to think for ourselves.

The [adapted manifesto extract](@/library/manifesto.en.md) introduces the relationship between intelligence, uncertainty, and action.
