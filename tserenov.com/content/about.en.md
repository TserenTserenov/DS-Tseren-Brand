I’m Tseren Tserenov, a co-founder of the Workshop for Engineers and Managers and the Aisystant platform. I write about systems thinking and personal development, work with a community of practitioners, and develop the Personal Development programme.

My interests centre on how people learn difficult things, change the way they work, and turn understanding into practice. These questions connect my writing, books, and talks.

## Writing and practice

My books and guides, written individually or with co-authors, cover systems thinking, systematic personal development, and the everyday practices that support it. Most of this body of work is currently available in Russian.

This English edition brings together selected adaptations for readers approaching these ideas for the first time. It will develop its own collection of essays and conversations.
